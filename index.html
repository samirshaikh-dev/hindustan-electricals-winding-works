Workflow & Quality Control

Our 5-Step Motor Rewinding Process

Every motor handled at our Vapi workshop passes through a strict electrical and mechanical standard to ensure long-term industrial reliability.

1

Inspection

Megger insulation test, surge test, bearing check, and root cause fault identification.

2

Dismantling

Careful core disassembly, coil burnout data logging, and slot cleaning.

3

Rewinding

Precision coil winding using super-enameled copper wire & Class F/H insulation.

4

Varnishing

Deep varnish impregnation and oven baking to eliminate moisture and vibration.

5

Testing

No-load current testing, resistance balance, and final quality sign-off.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions answered regarding motor rewinding, turnarounds, diagnostic procedures, and service capabilities.

Hindustan Electricals Winding Works rewinds and repairs AC single-phase and 3-phase induction motors, DC motors, generators and alternators, transformers, and welding machines in GIDC Vapi. We also handle pump motor rewinding, submersible pump repair, and blower motor winding for plants across Vapi, Silvassa, Daman, and Valsad.

Watch for abnormal overheating, frequent circuit breaker or overload trips, a burnt insulation smell, low Megger insulation readings, and failure to reach rated speed. If you see any of these, bring the motor to our workshop and we will test the winding to confirm the repair that is needed.

Cost depends on the motor's HP/kW rating, frame size, copper quantity, insulation class, and any mechanical work such as bearing or shaft repair. After an inspection we provide a clear estimate and an honest comparison with replacement cost before starting work.

Turnaround depends on the motor size, winding complexity, and current workload. We prioritize emergency breakdowns for manufacturing plants in Vapi GIDC and nearby industrial belts so you can return to production quickly.
