Vibration & Balance

Dynamic Rotor Balancing Service in Vapi

A motor can be rewound perfectly and still fail early if its rotor runs out of balance. Excessive vibration from an unbalanced rotor accelerates bearing wear, damages end-shields, causes shaft bending, and raises winding temperature. During motor overhauls and after rewinding, Hindustan Electricals Winding Works performs dynamic balancing of rotors and armatures so rebuilt motors run smooth, quiet, and long in demanding industrial duty.

Balancing & Vibration Services Provided

  • • Dynamic Rotor Balancing: Two-plane correction of motor rotors, squirrel-cage rotors, and wound armatures.
  • • Fan & Pulley Balancing: Balancing blower wheels, cooling fan assemblies, and motor-mounted pulleys.
  • • Vibration Diagnosis: Identifying whether vibration comes from unbalance, misalignment, or bad bearings.
  • • Balanced Reassembly: Correct keying, boring, and attitude checks so motors stay true at rated speed.

Our Balancing Workflow

  1. Vibration Assessment: Running the rotor and measuring shake, noise, and hot spots to confirm unbalance.
  2. Rotor Preparation: Cleaning, inspecting the shaft for truth, and checking fan and keyway condition.
  3. Spin Balancing: Rotating the assembly and locating the heavy spot in both correction planes.
  4. Weight Correction: Adding or removing balance material precisely until runout is minimized.
  5. Reassembly Check: Fitting bearings and end-shields, then verifying smooth running at operating speed.
