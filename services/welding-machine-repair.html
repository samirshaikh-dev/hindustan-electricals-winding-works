Welding Equipment

Heavy-Duty Welding Machine Repair & Rewinding in Vapi

Welding machines in fabrication shops and industrial maintenance yards are subjected to extreme duty cycles, short-circuit current surges, and workshop metal dust. When an ARC or transformer-based welding unit produces weak arc strike, excessive hum, erratic current control, or coil burnout, Hindustan Electricals Winding Works provides reliable repair and rewinding services.

Welding Machine Faults Repaired

  • • Burnt Secondary Copper Strips: High sustained amperage melting winding insulation.
  • • Defective Current Selector Switch: Rotary tap switch contacts sparking or fused shut.
  • • Excessive Vibration & Buzzing: Loose magnetic core clamping bolts or detached shunt chokes.
  • • Overheating & Thermal Trip: Blocked air pathways or internal insulation breakdown.

Our Repair Capabilities

We service both conventional oil-cooled and air-cooled industrial welding transformers:

  1. Primary & Secondary Coil Rewinding: Utilizing heavy-cross-section enameled copper strip and fiberglass wrapping.
  2. Insulation Overhaul: Replacing burnt pressboard, mica separators, and baked insulating varnish.
  3. Switch & Terminal Replacement: Installing heavy-duty brass output studs and multi-position tap selectors.
  4. Load & Arc Strike Testing: Simulating real load conditions with Megger safety insulation verification.
