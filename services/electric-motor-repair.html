Mechanical & Electrical Motor Overhaul in Vapi GIDC

Not every failing motor requires a full rewind. Often, mechanical issues such as worn bearings, shaft misalignment, broken cooling fans, or loose terminal blocks cause severe overheating, noise, or electrical tripping. At Hindustan Electricals Winding Works, we provide complete diagnostic motor repair services to fix the root cause and avoid repeat failures.

Key Mechanical & Electrical Repair Capabilities

  • • Precision Bearing Replacement: Installing premium grade C3 clearance bearings with proper hydraulic press fitment.
  • • Rotor Dynamic Balancing: Eliminating unbalance vibrations to extend bearing life and safeguard machine mountings.
  • • Shaft & Housing Sleeving: Machining worn rotor shafts and end-cover bearing housings back to standard tolerances.
  • • Terminal Block & Lead Wire Replacement: Upgrading brittle, charred terminal connections with heavy-duty heat-resistant leads.

Common Motor Symptoms & Solutions

Excessive Vibration & Grinding Noise

Caused by spalled bearing raceways, lack of lubrication, or mechanical misalignment. We dismantle, inspect housing bores, and install new precision bearings.

Rapid Overheating Under Normal Load

Often triggered by clogged ventilation shrouds, defective cooling impellers, or rotor bar cracks. We clean air passages and check rotor cage continuity.

Frequent Terminal Box Sparks or Burnout

Loose crimp lugs or moisture ingress lead to localized resistance and flashovers. We install new porcelain/Bakelite terminal blocks with silicone sealing.
