Industrial Alternator & Generator Repair in Vapi

Standby and continuous power diesel generator (DG) sets are essential for industrial operations in Vapi to prevent costly plant shutdowns during power outages. When an alternator produces zero output voltage, experiences fluctuating voltage under load, or suffers stator coil burnout, Hindustan Electricals Winding Works provides specialized generator winding and repair.

Generator & Alternator Services Provided

  • • Main Stator Rewinding: Precision replacement of 3-phase high-grade copper coils with Class H insulation.
  • • Exciter Stator & Rotor Rewinding: Overhauling auxiliary field excitation systems for stable voltage regulation.
  • • Rotating Diode & Varistor Testing: Inspecting and replacing surge-damaged rectifier assemblies.
  • • Slip Ring & Brush Gear Servicing: For brush-type generators, resurfacing brass rings and replacing carbon brushes.

Our Diagnostic & Rewinding Workflow

  1. Excitation Circuit Check: Verifying residual magnetism, AVR wiring, and rectifier diodes before assuming winding failure.
  2. Megger Insulation Test: Testing phase-to-phase and phase-to-ground insulation resistance at 1000V DC.
  3. Core Cleaning & Slot Insulation: Stripping burnt copper coils and lining slots with Nomex/Kapton insulation for extreme heat resilience.
  4. Precision Coil Winding: Re-inserting exact pitch and turn count windings using premium super-enameled copper wire.
  5. Thermal Varnish Impregnation & Baking: Protecting windings from oil fumes and humidity common in industrial generator rooms.
