Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026. How Hindustan Electricals Winding Works handles and protects customer information.

1. Introduction

At Hindustan Electricals Winding Works ("we", "our", or "us"), we value your privacy and are committed to protecting the personal and operational information shared with us through our website (hindustanelectricals.in), phone calls, or WhatsApp inquiries.

This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect when you request service quotes or consult with our motor rewinding workshop, how that information is used, and your rights regarding your data.

2. Information We Collect

We only collect information necessary to respond to your repair inquiries and provide quotes for electric motor rewinding and servicing:

  • • Contact Information: Your name, business or company name, contact phone number, and location.
  • • Motor Service Details: Motor type (AC/DC), horsepower/kW rating, make, voltage specifications, operating issues, or photos of motor nameplates.
  • • Communication History: Records of calls, WhatsApp messages, or form submissions relating to quotations, repairs, and job updates.

3. How We Use Your Information

The information we collect is strictly used for business and workshop operations:

  • • To evaluate your motor rewinding, AC/DC winding, or equipment repair requirements and provide accurate diagnostic quotes.
  • • To contact you via telephone or WhatsApp regarding the status of motor repairs, testing results, or job completion ready for pickup/delivery.
  • • To coordinate logistics, dispatch, or workshop visits at our Express House facility in GIDC Vapi.
  • • We do never sell, lease, or distribute your personal details to third-party marketing entities.

4. WhatsApp & Third-Party Communication

When you click to initiate a WhatsApp chat from our website, you connect directly with our workshop telephone number (+91 98252 72547) via WhatsApp's platform. Communications through WhatsApp are subject to WhatsApp's own privacy policy and terms of service.

5. Data Security

We implement appropriate organizational measures to safeguard your contact and service details against unauthorized access, alteration, or misuse. Internal access to customer information is restricted to authorized workshop technicians and administration personnel.
