About Hindustan Electricals Winding Works

Your trusted local partner for industrial motor rewinding, AC/DC motor repair, and electrical machinery maintenance in Vapi GIDC.

Our Identity

Dedicated Electric Motor Repair in Vapi, Gujarat

Hindustan Electricals Winding Works was established to provide reliable, precision-engineered motor rewinding and electrical repair services to the fast-growing industrial sector in Vapi and South Gujarat.

Industrial machinery downtime directly impacts production schedules and revenues. That's why our workshop focuses on methodical fault diagnosis, high-grade insulation materials, and prompt turnaround times. We handle standard single-phase motors, heavy 3-phase industrial motors, DC armatures, alternators, transformers, and industrial welding sets.

We operate transparently—logging original wire gauge, turn counts, and connection schemes for every motor before rewinding begins, guaranteeing that the rebuilt unit restores full operational torque and efficiency.
