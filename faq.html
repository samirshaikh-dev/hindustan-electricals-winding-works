Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions about electric motor rewinding, turnaround times, quality standards, and workshop capabilities.

We rewind and repair AC single-phase and 3-phase induction motors, DC motors, industrial generators and alternators, transformers, and welding machines. We also handle pump motor rewinding, submersible pump repair, blower motor winding, and rotor balancing from our workshop in GIDC Vapi.

Common warning signs are abnormal overheating, circuit breakers or overload relays tripping, a burnt or smoky smell from the motor, low insulation resistance (failed Megger test), and unsteady speed or phase current imbalance. If you notice any of these, bring the motor to our workshop and we will run a winding and insulation test to confirm whether it needs rewinding or a simpler repair.

A motor that fails repeatedly is usually caused by an external problem rather than the winding itself. Common causes include unbalanced supply voltage, overloading, single phasing, low voltage, moisture ingress, and blocked cooling. When we take in a motor we inspect the failure cause first, because rewinding without correcting the underlying fault will only burn the next winding too.

Cost depends mainly on the motor's HP/kW rating and frame size, the copper quantity, the insulation class used, and whether additional mechanical work such as shaft or bearing repair is required. There is no fixed price, so we inspect the motor and give you an estimate and an honest comparison with replacement cost before starting work.

For large industrial motors and non-standard units that are hard to source, rewinding is usually far more economical than buying new. For small, standard motors where a replacement is easily available and inexpensive, replacement may make more sense. After inspection we advise you honestly on which option gives better value.

Turnaround depends on the motor size, winding complexity, core condition, and our current workload. We prioritize emergency breakdowns for manufacturing plants in Vapi GIDC and nearby industrial belts to reduce downtime. Call us with your motor details and we will give you a realistic timeline.
