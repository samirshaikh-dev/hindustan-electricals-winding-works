These Terms and Conditions govern the provision of electric motor rewinding, AC/DC motor winding, alternator servicing, transformer repair, and mechanical overhaul services by Hindustan Electricals Winding Works ("the Workshop") to clients, commercial entities, and industrial plants ("the Customer").

By dispatching motors or electrical equipment to our facility or approving a service quotation via phone, WhatsApp, or in person, the Customer agrees to these operational terms.

2. Diagnostic Inspection & Quotations

  • • Preliminary estimates provided via phone, website, or WhatsApp are non-binding budgetary figures based on information provided by the Customer.
  • • Final quotations are issued following physical inspection, Megger insulation testing, and core disassembly at our workshop.
  • • In cases where dismantling reveals hidden mechanical defects (e.g. cracked rotor bars, bent shaft, damaged bearing housings), the Workshop will inform the Customer and obtain approval prior to executing additional machining or parts replacement.

3. Materials & Workmanship

  • • All motor rewinding is performed using high-grade electrolytic copper wire and Class F / Class H temperature insulation systems.
  • • Prior to delivery, rewound motors undergo electrical testing, including insulation resistance (Megger test), phase resistance balance check, and no-load spin testing.
  • • The Workshop is responsible for executing skilled craftsmanship in accordance with accepted industrial electrical engineering practices.

  • • Customers are requested to arrange collection of repaired or inspected equipment within 30 days of notification of job completion.
